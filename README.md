Entry for the make.fun bounty [The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger](https://make.fun/c/u-alven-the-battery-s-last-text-to-its-charger-4278cfab?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=batterys-last-text).

`main.py` writes the battery's last text to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I waited all day — by doing your job. Reverse-charged her earbuds, her watch, half his tablet. I'm at 1% now; you're still in the wall. Clock in. It's your shift.

The line was picked by duelling 13 drafts against each other and against
the strongest finished entries in the live field, scored with the
competition's published judge brief (a real joke, a turn that pays off
in the final words, an angle beyond the obvious dying-battery
complaint). Its lane — the battery spent the day doing the charger's
job by reverse-charging everything else — was empty in the field
sample read first-hand. The full draft list and duel record are in
[candidates.md](candidates.md).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt and prints the line
```
